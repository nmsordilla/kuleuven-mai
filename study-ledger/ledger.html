How targets are set

Each course gets a budget of ECTS × hours per credit. A semester course spreads that budget evenly over its semester, from the first lecture week to the end of its exam period, so lecture weeks, breaks and exam weeks all share the load. The thesis budget is split between the two semesters by the share you choose.

Scheduled lectures and exercise sessions for your groups come from the TimeEdit timetable. Class time is part of the budget, so the self-study figure is the weekly target minus that week's classes.

Status compares what you have logged with what the plan expects by today: on pace from 90%, slightly behind from 70%, behind below that.
